False dichotomy

Either this or that, with no third way

What it is

Presenting only two options when there are others. By hiding the alternatives, the argument forces a choice of the option that favors the speaker.

An analogy: "Either you agree with everything I say, or you are my enemy."

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this error appears in 67 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A1 Animals aren't conscious and don't feel pain like we do: Either it feels exactly like a human or it doesn't feel
  • A2 Fish and invertebrates don't feel pain: Either pure reflex or experience identical to ours
  • A3 Where do you draw the line? Insects? Bacteria?: "Either everything matters equally, or nothing matters"
  • A15 Animals aren't rational: Treats as binary (rational or irrational) something the evidence shows to be gradual
  • A26 Equating animals with disabled humans will degrade humans: Presents the protection of animals and of disabled people as rivals
  • B1 There is humane slaughter: "Die at the slaughterhouse or old and sick", as if not breeding animals to kill were not an option
  • B3 They had a good life and a quick death: Slaughterhouse or the wild, as if there were no other option
  • B16 It's a fair trade: we care for them and feed them, and they give us meat: Either the farm or cruel nature
  • D5 I don't want to dishonor my parents and my ancestors: Either I eat what my family eats, or I dishonor them
  • E5 Veganism is opinion, not science: Either it's science or it's opinion
  • E14 I admire you, but I can't do it: Either everything at once, or nothing
  • F1 On a desert island, would you eat an animal?: Either the rule is absolute or it's a preference
  • F10 Would you eat an animal that died of natural causes?: Either the vegan eats it, or their position is whim
  • F12 Would you eat animals if someone paid you well?: Either refuse at any price, or the value is fake
  • G18 Ultra-processed vegan foods are worse than meat: Either meat or ultra-processed vegan food
  • H7 Livestock pollutes little; the problem is fossil fuels: Either fossil fuels or food
  • H10 Cattle graze where crops can't grow: Either the land produces meat or it's good for nothing
  • H12 Agriculture can't work without manure: Either manure or no agriculture at all
  • H14 What matters is how food is grown, not whether it comes from animals: Either you look at the production method, or you look at the origin of the food
  • H16 Leather and wool are more sustainable than synthetics, which are plastic: Either leather or plastic
  • H18 Eating insects is the sustainable protein of the future: Meat or insects, forgetting plants, fermentation and cultivated meat
  • I1 Animal testing has saved millions of lives: Either animal testing or testing on children
  • I5 Medical and veterinary students need to practice on animals: Either practice on animals, or no practice
  • I6 Pig organs will save thousands of lives: Either pig organs or deaths on the list
  • I8 Leather is just a byproduct of meat: Either leather or plastic
  • I13 Carmine is a natural dye, better than artificial ones: Either carmine or artificial dye
  • I22 The fighting bull lives free in the field and dies fighting: Either bullfight or slaughterhouse
  • I28 Sport hunting funds conservation: Either hunting or no funding
  • I30 The cart is the carter's livelihood, and the horse is well cared for: Either the horse works, or the carter goes hungry
  • I31 Guide and assistance dogs are well cared for and do important work: Either veganism condemns everything, or it accepts everything
  • J3 Taxing meat or cutting subsidies hurts the poor: Either meat stays cheap or the poor go hungry
  • J4 It's hard; I don't have time: Either total, hard change or none at all
  • J5 Eating out, traveling and parties become impossible: Either meat or isolation
  • J12 I don't want to feel left out; eating together is how you belong: Either I eat the same as everyone, or I'm left out
  • J14 Vegans are against farmers: Either you accept the whole sector or you're against all of it
  • J16 This is my living; it feeds my family: Either everything is kept, or everything is lost
  • J20 There are more important human problems: Either humans or animals
  • K4 The problem is the industry, not consumers: "Either it's the system or it's the consumer"
  • K5 There is no ethical consumption under capitalism: Either systemic change or consumption
  • K6 Vegans should fight for laws, not diets: Either laws or diet
  • K7 Vegans shouldn't get into politics, because politics is dirty: Either purity outside politics, or corruption within it
  • K10 Vegans will always be a minority: Either veganism becomes the majority, or it changes nothing
  • L14 Vegans shouldn't use artificial intelligence: Either zero impact, or no right to criticise
  • L21 Vegans care more about animals than people: Either animals or humans
  • L24 Vegans are against religion: Either faith, or compassion for animals
  • L31 Defending animals is an emotional appeal, not an argument: Opposes emotion and reason, as if a position couldn't have both
  • L32 Defending animals is middle-class moralism: Assumes caring for animals rules out caring for humans
  • L35 My food beats yours: Sets "steak" against "lettuce", ignoring plant-based cooking
  • M3 Being vegan limited me when eating at my relatives' homes: Either refuse everything, or eat everything
  • M5 I stopped being vegan because I'm at a different stage of life: All or nothing, without considering doing what's possible
  • M6 Vegans are annoying and bossy; I don't want to be part of that group: Either I stay in the hostile group, or I go back to eating animals
  • M7 I don't want to be labeled; the label brings pressure: Either I'm "the vegan" under watch, or I eat everything
  • M8 It became an obsession; I lived with guilt: Either total vigilance, or total abandonment
  • M9 I was vegan and started getting too radical, too rigid: Either I'm rigid and vegan, or I'm flexible and go back to eating animals

In helping wild animals

  • N5 Helping the weak ruins natural selection: Either nature takes its course or the species degrades; ignores helping while controlling reproduction
  • N6 We didn't cause it, so we have no duty to help: Either the duty to help equals the duty not to harm, or it doesn't exist
  • N7 Domestic animals are our responsibility, wild ones are not: Either we have special duties or equal duties to everyone; ignores general duties plus special ones
  • N9 Meddling with nature is wrong; we could make everything worse: Either large-scale intervention without study, or nothing
  • N10 Using medicine and genetics to end suffering is playing God: Either we accept all suffering, or we eliminate all pain
  • N12 It's impossible to solve suffering in nature; it's too big and complex: Either we eliminate all suffering, or we do nothing
  • N13 We'd have to drop everything to help wild animals: Either you give your whole life, or you owe nothing
  • N15 Sentientists are against preserving species and biodiversity: Either biodiversity is the supreme value or it's worth nothing
  • N16 Sentientists want to get rid of forests: Either accept suffering in nature or get rid of nature
  • N17 Sentientists want to get rid of predators: Either everything stays as it is, or predators are eliminated
  • N18 Sentientists defend parasites: Sees only "defend the parasite" or "kill it", with no prevention or removal

In the long term and with new minds

  • N24 There's no way to plan for the long term, since we can't predict a distant future: Either you predict the scenario, or there is no rational action
  • N26 That's science fiction; we have real problems now: Either we take care of the present, or the future

When animal advocates make the same mistake

To be honest: animal advocates fall into it too. These items from Stop saying this show how, and what to say instead.

  • V13 Every vegan has to be friendly with non-vegans, or we won't convince anyone: There is a lot of truth here: hostile conversations tend to produce defensiveness and rejection, and someone who feels attacked stops listening.
  • V65 Anyone who won't accept gradual change doesn't understand how the world works: It mirrors the previous item, said by supporters of gradual change against those who call for an immediate end to exploitation.
  • V66 Vegans should focus only on direct activism, talking to people one by one: Talking to each person matters: it changes habits, makes new vegans and creates people who later support laws and campaigns.
  • V67 Vegans should focus only on institutional activism, changing laws, rules and court rulings: Changing laws, regulations and court rulings is what the available evidence supports most, and it is essential.
  • V68 Vegans should focus only on powerful and influential people: Influential people can change a lot at once, and some respond better to well-built arguments.
