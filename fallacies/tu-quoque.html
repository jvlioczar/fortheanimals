Tu quoque

"You do it too"

What it is

Answering a criticism by pointing out that the critic does something similar, instead of showing that the criticism is wrong. The speaker's inconsistency may be real, but it does not make the criticism false.

An analogy: A smoker warns that cigarettes are harmful, and the reply is "but you smoke".

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this error appears in 18 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • F5 What about the insects you kill while driving?: Accuses the other of doing something similar instead of answering
  • F6 You also kill mosquitoes and rats at home: Accuses the other instead of answering
  • H1 Vegans' soy is destroying the Amazon: Accuses vegans of something meat does on a far greater scale
  • H2 Harvests kill animals; vegans kill too: Accuses the other instead of comparing diets
  • H4 Plant monoculture destroys as much as industrial animal farming: "You destroy too" in place of comparing how much
  • H5 Avocados and almonds use a lot of water: Accuses vegans instead of comparing impacts
  • H6 Imported vegan food pollutes in transport: Accuses vegans instead of comparing impacts
  • I3 If you take medicine, you can't oppose testing: Accuses of inconsistency instead of answering
  • I35 Having a pet is exploitation too, and your cat eats meat: Accuses of inconsistency instead of answering
  • L11 You use a phone, so you're a hypocrite: Uses the speaker's conduct to reject what's said
  • L12 Veganism isn't 100% cruelty-free: Accuses vegans instead of comparing choices
  • L13 A vegan who buys from companies using slave labor isn't really vegan: The vegan's inconsistency is used to dismiss their argument
  • L14 Vegans shouldn't use artificial intelligence: The vegan's supposed failing becomes an excuse not to discuss the animals
  • L15 Vegans shouldn't watch violent films or play violent games: The vegan's supposed failing becomes an excuse not to discuss the animals
  • L16 Vegans are speciesists too: Uses the critic's bias to reject the criticism
  • L17 You don't feel empathy for every animal in the world either: Uses the limit of the vegan's empathy to reject what they say
  • L19 There are sexist, racist and transphobic vegans: Uses the vegan's failing in another cause to reject what they say about animals
  • L20 Vegans defend animals, but they support abortion: The vegan's supposed inconsistency becomes a reason to ignore animals

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
