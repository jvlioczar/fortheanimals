Straw man

Distorting the other person's position to refute it easily

What it is

Distorting or exaggerating the other side's position in order to refute it easily. What gets knocked down is the dummy, not the real position.

An analogy: Someone proposes less sugar in school lunches, and the reply is "so you want to ban children from eating sweets?".

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this error appears in 24 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A7 Vegans anthropomorphize animals: Denying similar mental states is also an error (anthropodenial), and the evidence contradicts it
  • C7 We are animals and part of the ecosystem; no one lives without impact: Attributes to veganism the promise of a life without impact
  • D15 Being vegan doesn't make you more spiritually evolved: Attributes to the vegan the thesis that they are superior, instead of the thesis that the harm is unjust
  • E2 The state can't ban what I eat: Attributes to vegans a proposal for an immediate ban
  • E13 He eats meat, but he's a good person: Attributes to veganism the accusation that meat eaters are evil
  • F4 Should we stop lions from hunting them, then?: Attributes to vegans a proposal to lock up predators
  • G19 Fruitarianism isn't healthy, so veganism isn't either: Attacks fruitarianism and concludes against veganism
  • J14 Vegans are against farmers: Attributes to veganism an opposition to crop growing that it doesn't have
  • L8 Veganism is moral puritanism: A label in place of a rebuttal: veganism is about harm to third parties, not the purity of the eater, and every morality goes against natural impulses
  • L11 You use a phone, so you're a hypocrite: Treats veganism as a rejection of technology or modern life
  • L12 Veganism isn't 100% cruelty-free: Attributes a promise of purity to veganism
  • L17 You don't feel empathy for every animal in the world either: Assumes the defense of animals depends on feeling empathy for all of them
  • L18 If vegans eat fake meat, they want meat: Attributes to veganism a rejection of taste
  • L25 Veganism is just a fad diet: Treats an ethical position as a menu
  • L29 Vegans treat vegetarians as enemies: Attributes to veganism the thesis that vegetarians are worse than meat eaters
  • L31 Defending animals is an emotional appeal, not an argument: Swaps the argument for the campaign that spreads it
  • L33 Animal advocates want to get rid of pets: Swaps "stop breeding new animals" for "take animals out of homes"

In helping wild animals

  • N13 We'd have to drop everything to help wild animals: Attacks the idea that each person must save animals with their own hands, which no one proposed
  • N15 Sentientists are against preserving species and biodiversity: Attributes to the sentientist a wish to lose biodiversity
  • N16 Sentientists want to get rid of forests: Swaps "research and help" for "destroy"
  • N17 Sentientists want to get rid of predators: Attributes to the position the conclusion it rejects
  • N18 Sentientists defend parasites: Incomplete count: without treatment many parasites die with the host, death is only a harm to those who feel and would have a good life, and preventing is not killing
  • N19 Sentientists support population control and antinatalism for wild animals: A case-by-case assessment becomes a doctrine of "less life, always"
  • N20 Sentientists give the same weight to insects, invertebrates and mammals: Attributes to the opponent a position they don't hold

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
