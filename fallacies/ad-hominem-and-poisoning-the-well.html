Ad hominem and poisoning the well

Attacking the person

What it is

Attacking the person who presents an argument instead of the argument. In poisoning the well, the person is discredited before speaking, so that no one takes seriously what they will say.

An analogy: Rejecting a math calculation because the person who did it is unpleasant.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this error appears in 25 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A7 Vegans anthropomorphize animals: Dismisses the person as sentimental instead of discussing the evidence
  • B14 Those videos are exceptions or staged: Discredits whoever filmed instead of discussing what was filmed
  • B15 You don't live on a farm, so you don't know the truth: Assesses who speaks, not what is said
  • D15 Being vegan doesn't make you more spiritually evolved: Responds to the speaker's attitude, not to the argument
  • E7 Defending animals is just utilitarianism: Attacks Singer instead of the argument
  • J18 Billions of people depend on fish for protein: "Oversensitive" discredits the speaker
  • J21 People are dying in a war and you're worried about animals: Swaps the subject for a judgement of the speaker's character
  • L1 Vegans are annoying and think they are better: Attacks the person instead of the argument
  • L2 Vegans are all hippies: Attacks the image of the advocate, not the argument
  • L3 Vegans are crazy; they have mental problems: Judges the idea by the health of whoever holds it
  • L4 Sentientists are even crazier than vegans: Replaces the argument with a label about whoever holds it
  • L5 Vegan activists are radicals and extremists: Judging a cause by the methods of a minority
  • L6 Meat is for real men: A gender stereotype; masculinity is neither a moral nor a nutritional criterion
  • L7 Veganism is a cult, a religion: A label used to avoid evaluating; veganism rests on public arguments
  • L8 Veganism is moral puritanism: The puritan label discredits the vegan before the argument
  • L11 You use a phone, so you're a hypocrite: Attacks the person instead of the argument
  • L19 There are sexist, racist and transphobic vegans: Assesses the person in place of the argument
  • L25 Veganism is just a fad diet: Discredits those who join, not the argument
  • L26 Vegans support pseudoscience: The gullibility attributed to the advocate replaces examining the argument
  • L27 Vegan documentaries are manipulated: Calling it "propaganda" excuses one from examining what's shown
  • L29 Vegans treat vegetarians as enemies: Rejects the criticism of milk and eggs because of the tone of whoever makes it
  • L30 Vegans think they're the only ones who really defend animals: The arrogance of some becomes a reason to ignore the idea
  • L31 Defending animals is an emotional appeal, not an argument: Dismisses the idea because of the emotional state of whoever defends it
  • L37 I hate vegans: Rejects the idea because of who defends it
  • M6 Vegans are annoying and bossy; I don't want to be part of that group: Rejects the practice because of the behavior of those who defend it

When animal advocates make the same mistake

To be honest: animal advocates fall into it too. These items from Stop saying this show how, and what to say instead.

  • V6 Meat eaters are psychopaths: It's inaccurate and counterproductive. Most of these people have genuine emotional bonds.
  • V48 Doctors don't know anything about nutrition: It's a generalization. Nutrition training in medical school is often short, but that doesn't mean no doctor understands the subject, and many specialize in it.
  • V79 Veganism that doesn't discuss racism is just rich kids' cuisine: The concern has a basis. Racism can affect who has access to healthy food and who does the hardest jobs in the meat chain, and Black authors such as A.
