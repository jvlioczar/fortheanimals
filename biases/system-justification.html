System justification

Legitimizing the existing order to reduce discomfort

What it is

The tendency to defend and legitimize the way society is already organized, seeing it as fair and necessary, even when it harms someone, sometimes even those who defend it.

An analogy: Someone who says an old, unequal company rule "must have a good reason" without knowing what it is.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 25 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A9 Humans are superior, so we can use the inferior: Leads people to see the existing order as natural and deserved
  • A14 Humans have intrinsic value; animals have instrumental value: The dominant philosophical tradition seems proof of the conclusion
  • A21 No duties, no rights: The current legal arrangement comes to be read as the correct one
  • A22 Rights are a human invention, only for humans: Leads people to treat current institutions as just because they're current
  • A24 Animals are property: The current legal arrangement seems the correct arrangement
  • A25 Treating animals well is a matter of compassion, not justice: The current legal arrangement seems the natural boundary of justice
  • B1 There is humane slaughter: An official rule existing makes the practice seem legitimate
  • B8 Stressed animals make bad meat, so farmers take good care of them: The market seems to guarantee the right outcome
  • B10 The law guarantees animal welfare: The existing order seems fair because it is official
  • B14 Those videos are exceptions or staged: Official production seems right because it is official
  • C9 Might makes right: An order in which humans dominate seems natural and fair
  • C10 Farm animals exist for this: Current use seems like the natural order of things
  • D1 We've always done it: The established order comes to seem fair because it's established
  • D9 It's legal; the law allows it: The legal order seems fair because it's official
  • D10 God gave us dominion over animals: The current order seems willed by God
  • H8 Cow methane is biogenic; it doesn't count: Livestock farming seems a natural part of the economy
  • H10 Cattle graze where crops can't grow: Livestock farming seems the natural order of the countryside
  • H19 Hunting controls populations: Culling seems the natural form of management
  • I8 Leather is just a byproduct of meat: The industry seems to use everything responsibly
  • I15 Zoos conserve species and educate: A traditional institution seems necessary
  • I17 Aquariums and dolphin parks are harmless: A traditional attraction seems harmless
  • J13 Agriculture is the backbone of the economy: The dominant sector seems necessary and legitimate
  • J14 Vegans are against farmers: The dominant sector seems necessary and untouchable as a whole
  • J15 Farmers and slaughterhouse workers would lose their jobs: The sector seems essential and untouchable
  • K8 In a democracy, only legal means of change are legitimate: Existing rules seem legitimate because they exist

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
