Hypocrisy hunting

Looking for an inconsistency in whoever defends an idea so as not to examine the idea.

What it is

Looking for inconsistencies in those who advocate a change in order to feel excused from changing. Finding a flaw in the messenger relieves the pressure of the message.

An analogy: Ignoring the doctor's advice to stop drinking because the doctor has a glass of wine.

How it shows up against the case for animals
