Functional categorization

"Food" animals are granted less mind than "pet" animals

What it is

Attributing abilities and value to an animal according to the use we make of it: animals classified as "food" are credited with less mind and less consideration than "companion" animals, even when they are similar.

An analogy: Seeing the same piece of furniture as valuable in the living room and as trash on the curb, just because of the category it was put in.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 20 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A7 Vegans anthropomorphize animals: "Food" animals are granted less mind than "pet" animals
  • A8 They're just animals: Animals labeled as food lose perceived moral status
  • A15 Animals aren't rational: Classifying an animal as "food" reduces the perception that it suffers
  • A20 Animals aren't persons: Animals classed as food lose the traits we associate with someone
  • A24 Animals are property: "Production" animals are seen as things, and "pets" as someone
  • A25 Treating animals well is a matter of compassion, not justice: "Production" animals seem less worthy of protection than pets
  • B6 Veal makes use of the calves born in dairy farming: The calf is seen as a byproduct, not as an individual
  • C10 Farm animals exist for this: "Food" animals are granted less mind and less moral status
  • C11 Cows need to be milked: The cow is seen as a "dairy cow", not as a mother
  • E4 Eating meat isn't an ideology; veganism is: "Food" seems like a property of the animal
  • E11 I love animals, but...: "Food" and "companion" seem like different kinds of animal
  • F9 What about the eggs from my rescued hen?: The hen is still seen as an "egg hen"
  • F10 Would you eat an animal that died of natural causes?: Farm animals are seen as food in any situation
  • F11 What about meat that gets thrown away?: Meat is still seen as food, not as an animal
  • H15 If meat is sustainable, there's no problem: The animal is seen as a product, not as someone
  • H20 Kangaroo meat is the most sustainable meat there is: A kangaroo seen as "pest" or "meat" loses moral weight
  • I10 Sheep need to be shorn: The sheep is seen as a "wool sheep"
  • I29 Horses like working and being ridden: The horse is seen as a "working animal"
  • I35 Having a pet is exploitation too, and your cat eats meat: Adopting and caring differs from breeding to use; cats need nutrients, not meat; and none of this licenses eating animals
  • L30 Vegans think they're the only ones who really defend animals: "Pet" and "food" seem like different kinds of animal

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
