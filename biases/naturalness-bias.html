Naturalness bias

What seems natural is judged better and safer.

What it is

Preferring what is considered natural and distrusting what is considered artificial, even when there is no difference in quality or safety. It is the psychological counterpart of the appeal to nature.

An analogy: Preferring a "natural" juice with more sugar to one with sweetener, assuming it is automatically healthier.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 27 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • C1 It's natural to eat meat: What is perceived as natural is judged better and safer
  • C2 We have canine teeth; we are omnivores: What seems natural is seen as better
  • C7 We are animals and part of the ecosystem; no one lives without impact: What is described as part of nature seems right or untouchable
  • F4 Should we stop lions from hunting them, then?: What happens in nature seems good or untouchable
  • G1 Eating animals is necessary to survive: What the species did for a long time seems obligatory
  • G4 If you need B12 supplements, it isn't natural: What seems natural is seen as healthier
  • G18 Ultra-processed vegan foods are worse than meat: Meat seems "natural" even when processed
  • H16 Leather and wool are more sustainable than synthetics, which are plastic: Natural material seems greener
  • I9 A fur coat is natural and comes from regulated farms: What's natural seems better
  • I11 Silk and down are natural and don't harm animals: What's natural seems harmless
  • I13 Carmine is a natural dye, better than artificial ones: What comes from nature seems good and safe
  • I37 Feeding a dog or cat vegan food is animal abuse: What the animal would eat in the wild always seems best
  • L8 Veganism is moral puritanism: What goes against custom seems to go against nature
  • L14 Vegans shouldn't use artificial intelligence: What is natural seems good in itself

In helping wild animals

  • N1 Wild animals live free and happy: The natural is seen as good and healthy
  • N2 Suffering and death are part of nature: The natural seems good or inevitable
  • N3 A balanced nature is good for animals: Natural balance seems good in itself
  • N5 Helping the weak ruins natural selection: What comes from nature seems good and safe without needing justification
  • N8 Who are we to interfere with nature?: The wild is seen as untouchable and good
  • N9 Meddling with nature is wrong; we could make everything worse: The natural seems balanced and good
  • N10 Using medicine and genetics to end suffering is playing God: The natural seems sacred or untouchable
  • N14 Helping wild animals isn't veganism; it's a different cause: The natural seems outside the moral domain
  • N15 Sentientists are against preserving species and biodiversity: An ecosystem's "original" state seems good in itself
  • N16 Sentientists want to get rid of forests: Questioning whether nature is good for animals sounds like attacking it
  • N17 Sentientists want to get rid of predators: What is natural seems good, and changing it seems wrong in itself
  • N18 Sentientists defend parasites: What happens in nature seems right and better than intervention
  • N19 Sentientists support population control and antinatalism for wild animals: What is natural is taken to be good

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
