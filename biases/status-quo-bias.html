Status quo bias

Preferring the current state because it is current

What it is

Preferring things to stay as they are just because that is the current state, and demanding more proof from any change than from what already exists.

An analogy: Keeping an expensive phone plan for years because switching seems like a hassle, even knowing a better one exists.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 60 arguments. Each links to the full answer.
