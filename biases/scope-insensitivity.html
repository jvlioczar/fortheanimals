Scope insensitivity

Billions of victims don't weigh more than one

What it is

Failing to scale concern to the size of the problem: the reaction to a harm affecting millions or billions is almost the same as to one affecting a few.

An analogy: Giving almost the same amount to save two thousand birds or two hundred thousand, as in classic studies on the subject.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 27 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A2 Fish and invertebrates don't feel pain: Trillions of victims produce no more emotion than one
  • A3 Where do you draw the line? Insects? Bacteria?: Billions of clearly sentient animals get diluted in a discussion about bacteria
  • A4 Plants feel too: Ignores that farming consumes far more plants
  • A10 What matters is the species, not the individual: Billions of individuals become a statistical abstraction
  • B1 There is humane slaughter: Billions of slaughters weigh no more than the image of one well-done slaughter
  • B4 Free-range animals are happy: They're still killed young; labels have little to do with practice, and changing the scale doesn't change the act
  • F8 What if they discover that plants feel?: Ignores the difference in the quantity of plants consumed
  • H2 Harvests kill animals; vegans kill too: Animal farming uses far more cropland (for feed) and land
  • H18 Eating insects is the sustainable protein of the future: Trillions of victims weigh no more than millions
  • J9 I swapped red meat for chicken, which is healthier: One chicken or two hundred look the same on the plate
  • J11 I'm pescatarian; I swapped meat for fish and spare the animals: Very large numbers lose their weight
  • J18 Billions of people depend on fish for protein: Trillions of fish become tonnes and percentages
  • J21 People are dying in a war and you're worried about animals: The number of animals killed in a conflict produces no proportional response
  • K1 One person doesn't make a difference: Billions of animals are perceived as an abstraction, and a single animal seems to change nothing
  • K12 The reason doesn't matter, health and the environment convince more people: Counts kilos of meat, not the number of individuals killed
  • K13 Everyone helps the cause they care about; you can't compare them: Billions of victims weigh no more than a thousand when choosing
  • L14 Vegans shouldn't use artificial intelligence: Small, visible impacts weigh more than large, diffuse ones
  • L17 You don't feel empathy for every animal in the world either: Billions of animals weigh no more than one
  • L23 Vegans are racist, because they criticize Afro-Brazilian religions: Billions of ordinary slaughters weigh less than the few ritual ones

In helping wild animals

  • N3 A balanced nature is good for animals: Billions of deaths of the young become statistics
  • N12 It's impossible to solve suffering in nature; it's too big and complex: Huge numbers stop moving us and start paralyzing us
  • N17 Sentientists want to get rid of predators: The number of animals who die in nature produces no proportional response
  • N20 Sentientists give the same weight to insects, invertebrates and mammals: The difference between millions and trillions of victims barely changes what we feel

In the long term and with new minds

  • N22 Those not yet born don't matter: Astronomical numbers stop moving us
  • N23 We have to worry about future humans, not about animals: Very large numbers fail to produce a proportional response
  • N24 There's no way to plan for the long term, since we can't predict a distant future: Enormous numbers fail to produce a proportional response
  • N25 The future will certainly be better, so there's no need to worry so much: Rising numbers of victims fail to produce a proportional response

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
