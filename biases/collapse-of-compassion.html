Collapse of compassion

The more victims, the less we feel for each; one identifiable victim moves us more than millions.

What it is

The more victims there are, the less one feels for each. One identifiable victim moves people more than millions described in numbers.

An analogy: A photo of one child moves people more than the news that thousands are starving.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 15 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • A10 What matters is the species, not the individual: Species don't feel; individuals do
  • F5 What about the insects you kill while driving?: Small, numerous animals stop seeming like individuals
  • H18 Eating insects is the sustainable protein of the future: The more victims, the less we feel for each one
  • I6 Pig organs will save thousands of lives: The organ shortage is real, but there are paths that do not breed animals for their organs: human donation, artificial and grown organs
  • J21 People are dying in a war and you're worried about animals: The animals are victims of the same war; the cost of helping them is far smaller than the sentence assumes, and a cost existing decides nothing, since that holds for every use of resources
  • K1 One person doesn't make a difference: Billions of deaths don't move us as one does
  • K13 Everyone helps the cause they care about; you can't compare them: Numerous, faceless victims move us less than one identifiable victim
  • L17 You don't feel empathy for every animal in the world either: The more victims, the less we feel for each one
  • M8 It became an obsession; I lived with guilt: Faced with billions of victims, the mind tires and shuts down

In helping wild animals

  • N1 Wild animals live free and happy: The adults we see are the exception: in most species, almost all young die shortly after birth, from hunger, cold, disease or predation
  • N7 Domestic animals are our responsibility, wild ones are not: A special relationship can give priority, but it doesn't erase the duty to others; we help distant humans with no relationship to us
  • N13 We'd have to drop everything to help wild animals: The larger the number of victims, the less weight each one gets, and the problem seems too big to be worth it
  • N15 Sentientists are against preserving species and biodiversity: Millions of animals killed in environmental programs don't move us the way one does

In the long term and with new minds

  • N22 Those not yet born don't matter: Whoever is born will feel what we leave them: that's why burying toxic waste is wrong, even if the victims don't exist yet
  • N23 We have to worry about future humans, not about animals: It has already accepted the hard part, that future beings count; what is missing is why, once that future arrives, only the humans in it would count, and most of those who will feel will not be human

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
