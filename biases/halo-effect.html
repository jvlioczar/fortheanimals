Halo effect

One good quality, like "local" or "organic", colors the judgment of everything else.

What it is

Letting one positive quality of someone or something influence the assessment of all their other qualities, with no link between them.

An analogy: Assuming that a good-looking person must also be honest and competent.

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 12 arguments. Each links to the full answer.

In the exploitation and use of animals

  • B12 Foie gras is a traditional delicacy, and ducks naturally eat a lot: The prestige of "traditional" makes the product seem good in every way and hides the current method
  • E13 He eats meat, but he's a good person: The person's general goodness extends to what they eat
  • H4 Plant monoculture destroys as much as industrial animal farming: "Regenerative" and "local" make the system seem good in every respect
  • H13 Local, organic meat is sustainable: The "organic" label makes the product seem better in every way
  • H14 What matters is how food is grown, not whether it comes from animals: "Regenerative" makes the product seem good in every respect
  • H17 Sustainable fishing and aquaculture solve it: The label makes the product seem good in every way
  • I12 Bees make extra honey: Honey seems natural and healthy in every way
  • I14 I only use cruelty-free products: An ethical label makes the whole product seem ethical
  • I16 Without zoos, several species would already be extinct: Some programs' work lends legitimacy to the whole institution
  • I32 Police and detection dogs are well cared for and save lives: Admiration for the work makes the cost to the dog invisible
  • J9 I swapped red meat for chicken, which is healthier: "Healthy" makes the choice look good in every sense

In helping wild animals

  • N15 Sentientists are against preserving species and biodiversity: The word "conservation" makes any method look good

When animal advocates make the same mistake

The Stop saying this catalogue does not yet record an example of this error among animal advocates. That does not mean it does not happen: the same care applies to both sides.
