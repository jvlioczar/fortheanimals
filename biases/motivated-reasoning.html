Motivated reasoning

Looking for reasons for the conclusion you already want

What it is

Assessing arguments and evidence according to the conclusion one wants, rather than their quality. Arguments that favor what we want seem strong; opposing ones seem weak.

An analogy: The fan who sees a penalty in every foul against their team and none in fouls in its favor.

How it shows up against the case for animals
