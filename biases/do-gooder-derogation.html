Do-gooder derogation

Belittling people who act for moral reasons, because their conduct feels like an accusation.

What it is

Devaluing or ridiculing those who act more ethically, because their example makes others feel judged, even when they judge no one.

An analogy: Mocking the coworker who returns extra change, calling them a "goody-goody".

How it shows up against the case for animals

In the catalogue, this bias supports 23 arguments. Each links to the full answer.
