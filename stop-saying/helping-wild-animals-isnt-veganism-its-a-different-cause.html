V75

"Helping wild animals isn't veganism, it's a different cause."

The cause as a tool

They use the animal movement for another purpose, or pretend two goals never conflict.

Why it is weak

By definition, that is true: veganism is about not exploiting animals. But the reason not to exploit them is that animals feel, and that reason doesn't change depending on who caused the suffering. An animal with hunger, disease or parasites feels it just the same.

Said by vegans, the line defines veganism against its own foundation and keeps a hierarchy among animals based on where the harm comes from, a form of speciesism that survives even among people who have already rejected exploitation.

Nor is there any need to choose. You can fight animal farming and support research and help for wild animals, such as rescues during fires and floods, which most vegans already approve of.

How to say it better

Keep the definition of veganism and say where it comes from: if what matters is sentience, animals who suffer in nature matter too, even if helping them is a cause with its own name.

Choosing a movement's focus is legitimate, and many vegans prefer to concentrate on ending exploitation. What this entry questions is the step from focus to the idea that the rest doesn't matter. The objection has its own chapter in the guide, in the wild animals family.
