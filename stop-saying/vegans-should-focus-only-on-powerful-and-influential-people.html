V58

"Vegans should focus only on powerful and influential people."

Purity and invented rules

They create demands veganism does not make and push away people who might join, without helping a single animal.

Why it is weak

Influential people can change a lot at once, and some respond better to well-built arguments. But research on the question points to no consensus in favour of focusing on them.

They are harder to reach and contested by many other causes, and public opinion usually decides whether a policy passes and whether it lasts.

A cause supported only at the top is fragile: it changes when those people change their minds or their positions.

How to say it better

Approach influential people when it makes sense, without abandoning the broad public. Influence and popular support reinforce each other.

All these forms of activism matter, and they reinforce each other: laws need public support to pass and to last, and personal change gains strength when rules and surroundings help. That doesn't mean they all weigh the same. There may well be one worth concentrating more effort on, and it should be chosen by comparing the animal cause with other justice movements and looking at the research on what works, not by personal preference.
