V9

"We have to confront non-vegans, since they're the extremists."

Exaggeration or attack

They claim more than the evidence supports, or attack people instead of discussing what they do.

Why it is weak

Part of the sentence has a basis: confronting an unjust practice is legitimate, and thinking it wrong to kill animals needlessly is not an extreme position.

The problem is the method. A very aggressive approach can push away people who could become vegan and cause a backlash, in which the person digs into the opposite position. Labeling non-vegans extremists also swaps the argument for an attack.

Confronting is not the same as attacking: you can be direct, firm and honest about the facts without humiliating anyone. In some cases, such as exposés and public campaigns, a harder stance may be used, but the context and the effect have to be weighed very carefully.

How to say it better

Be firm with the argument and respectful with the person. Keep the harder confrontation for situations where there is reason to expect it to help, and assess the results.

This item and the previous one are opposites: neither tone works every time, and the most useful thing is to adjust the tone to the context and check the effect.
