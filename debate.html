Simulated debate

Someone opens with one of the most common arguments. You choose the answer, and the conversation goes where these debates usually go. At the end, see how you did.

Each round has a strong answer, a weak one (exaggerated, biased or swapping the ethical question for another) and a bad one. The strong answers summarize the guide's chapters; the weak and bad ones come largely from the arguments vegans should stop using. There are 87 playable arguments, linked by the anti-vegan line of reasoning.
