"Comparing speciesism to racism or ableism is offensive; they are different things."

Anti-racism and anti-ableism: the principle

Do not give less weight to someone's interests because of a trait that does not change how much they can be harmed, such as race, disability, origin or appearance.

Why excluding animals contradicts the principle

  • The comparison is not between victims but between structures of reasoning: in every case, a trait irrelevant to the harm is used to discount interests (A8, A11).
  • Ableism and speciesism share a criterion: treating cognitive ability as the measure of worth. Authors such as Sunaura Taylor, who lives with a disability, discuss this link in Beasts of Burden.
  • Each struggle has its own history and its own victims, and none needs to be diluted. But the principle that sustains them does not stop at the species line.

The strongest counter-reply

The most serious version says these comparisons have been used to animalize and dehumanize human groups, and should therefore be avoided.

The answer

The caution is fair, and the argument for animals stands without analogies (L21). But the problem with dehumanization was always treating someone as if they did not matter; the consistent answer is that no one who feels should be treated that way.

Who defends animals from within this theory

Authors, activists and groups who start from this theory and show why it has to include animals and other sentient beings.

  • Sunaura Taylor: American artist and writer with a disability; in Beasts of Burden (2017), she links animal liberation to disability liberation
  • Aph Ko and Syl Ko: American writers; in Aphro-ism (2017), they discuss racism and speciesism from Black thought
  • A. Breeze Harper: American scholar; she edited Sistah Vegan (2010), with writing by Black vegan women
  • Marjorie Spiegel: author of The Dreaded Comparison (1988), on the similarities between human slavery and animal exploitation
  • Angela Davis: American philosopher and activist; a vegan, she has argued in lectures that animal rights and human rights are linked
